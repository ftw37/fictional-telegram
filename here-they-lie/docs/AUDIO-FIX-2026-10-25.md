# Audio correction — 2026-10-25

The previous HTL compatibility setting treated NGS2 port volume and Audio3D object gain as decibels. Live commands instead contain linear fades such as 0, 0.0025, 0.067, 0.223 and 0.447. The conversion promoted mute (0) to full volume (1), and promoted tiny fades to almost full volume. This explains why the background remained loud and masked dialogue.

Both gain paths now preserve nonnegative finite linear values. Negative/nonfinite values are silent. Legacy decibel overrides remain explicit diagnostic options, disabled normally. Both paths must be corrected together: the earlier one-path tests were incomplete and did not establish a fix.

The game's custom sampler filter module 0x13 is now processed by the existing biquad implementation rather than rejected. Its process-callback layout and 0x20 finish/unreserve flag were independently checked against the installed game's callback, so no speculative stop flags or fixed train timeouts were added.

Linked-channel output peak protection adds headroom without changing panning or boosting quiet signals. It acts before the playback backend's user/game volume. Normal rendering flags, GPU synchronization, occlusion counters, readbacks, pipeline caches and game assets are unchanged. No renderer source was modified for this audio work.

Verification so far: native overload/silence/nonfinite/stereo-ratio tests, positional left/right mirror and multi-block tests, and 12 Python tests pass. The corrected opening capture yielded multiple coherent dialogue phrases in offline Vosk recognition, with many words at model confidence 1.0; the earlier mix yielded only a fragment. Recognition is supporting evidence, not a physical listening/Quest test. No recordings were uploaded.

Three exact-binary routes now passed: audio-linear-both-repeat-20261025 (recorded), audio-normal-perf-1-20261025 and audio-normal-perf-2-20261025 (no recording/traces). All platform endpoints were visually checked. The interrupted run is excluded. Native binary SHA256: e15671da356ec81e937ffb871d1935f102c278916380dfad5aa1e6587579a660.

These results show no consistent regression in the sampled stages, not a guarantee of identical frame times or sustained 60 FPS. Station/loading variability remains. The old executable used a dummy SDL audio device to prevent its excessive audio playing; corrected runs retained normal audio processing with zeroed final device buffers. This is a practical rendering comparison, not a perfectly identical audio-backend benchmark.

The accepted final device-bound stereo capture is 418.112 seconds, with zero full-scale samples and -12.04 dBFS peaks before backend/user volume. Dialogue-window RMS L/R: -25.43/-27.68 dBFS; platform-tail RMS: -25.03/-30.48 dBFS. Intermediate float-to-PCM diagnostic dumps can still reach full scale and should not be confused with the protected final stream. No claim of fixed source mastering follows from a final limiter alone; corrected game fades are the principal balance repair.

Offline recognition on the same opening span improved from 4 words (none at confidence>=0.9) with positional dB interpretation to 48 words (34 at confidence>=0.9) with both gains linear. Several coherent dialogue phrases were recovered. This supports intelligibility without claiming a physical speaker/headset listening test. The recognizer/model came from the official [Vosk project](https://alphacephei.com/vosk/models), ran locally, and was removed afterward.
