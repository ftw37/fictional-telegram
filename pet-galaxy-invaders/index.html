Move left / right (arrow keys also work)

Change parameters

Clear every invader to begin a new wave. Shelters stop both sides’ shots, but wear away. The passing mystery ship has a random bonus.

A browser recreation of the PET program titled Galaxy Invaders, supplied as space invader.prg. Its original launcher and machine-code program are preserved in this project. Graphics, scoring and timing are adapted for the browser.
