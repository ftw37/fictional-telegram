Destroy all enemy men to begin a new rack. The game ends when all your men are killed or the enemy overruns you. The mystery ship has a high random value.

The uploaded Galaxy Invaders arcade program runs as original 6502 machine code. Every screen cell comes from its 40-column PET character display, using the PET uppercase/graphics character set. A small adapter supplies keyboard input and interrupts; it is not a full PET emulator. Arrow keys, touch controls, pause and optional approximate CB2 sound are browser additions.
